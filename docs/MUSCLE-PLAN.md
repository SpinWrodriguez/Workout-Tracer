# The 8-week muscle plan, set up by hand

No plan feature: these are workouts you create once in the app, give a usual
weekday, and repeat. What changes week to week is the sets, and you edit that
twice. Golf is Saturday, so Thursday and Friday carry no heavy legs and no
heavy grip; Friday is light upper only.

## What the last six weeks say

20 sessions across 12 different workouts, about 3.3 a week. Because the
workouts kept changing, almost nothing was repeated often enough to progress:

| | Sessions | Went from → to | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smith calf raise | 8 | 18 → 48 kg | The most-trained lift in the block. Calves do not need this. |
| Pull-up | 6 | 6 reps → 6 reps | Stuck: body weight only, no way to add load. |
| Back squat | 4 | 35 → 50 kg × 10 | Progressing well. Keep. |
| Bench press | 3 | 40 → 47.5 kg | Progressing. Keep. |
| Romanian deadlift | 2 | 20 → 35 kg | Far too light next to the squat, and only twice. **The biggest gap.** |
| Rows (any) | 4 | 50 kg | Upper back under-trained. |
| Shoulder press | 2 | 15 kg | Delts barely trained. Lateral raises: 5 kg, once. |
| Hamstring curl, hip thrust | 0 | — | Never done. Hamstrings only via RDL; glutes only via squats. |
| Pallof, Copenhagen, landmine rotation | 5 / 5 / 3 | progressing | The golf work is the one thing done consistently. Good. |

So: the squat and bench are the only two lifts with a thread. The plan gives
every main lift that thread: the same lift, every week, for eight weeks.

## The workouts

Every workout opens the same way (about 10 minutes): 5 min warm-up (90/90
hip switch, open book, T-spine + high knee, not logged), then one explosive
or rotational move 3 × 5 fast, then one anti-rotation move 2 × 10. Then the
lifting. Sets below are **week 1**; the table further down changes them.

### E · Golf primer — the optional 5th day, Friday (30 min, light)

Your existing **Golf Rotation Power** workout as it is, at 2 sets each, with
C moved to Thursday that week. Rotation and mobility only, nothing heavy.

### Backup · One day — any day (45 min)

For a week where only one day is possible. The main lift of each workout,
three sets each, so every lift still gets its weekly exposure and the weight
still goes up next week:

Jump squat 2 × 5 · Pallof 2 × 10 · Back squat 3 × 8 · Lat pulldown 3 × 10 ·
Bench press 3 × 8 · Romanian deadlift 3 × 10 (Mon–Wed) **or** hip thrust 3 × 10
(Thu–Fri) · Dumbbell shoulder press 2 × 10.

Two days possible: do A and C, and C takes the pulldown from B.

## How a week goes

| Days you have | Do |
|---|---|
| 3 | A Mon · B Wed · C Fri |
| 4 | A Mon · B Tue · D Wed · C Thu or Fri |
| 5 | A Mon · B Tue · D Wed · C Thu · E Fri |
| 2 | A, then C with the pulldown added |
| 1 | Backup |
| 0 | Nothing to do. Next week carries on; don't try to make it up. |

A missed workout moves to the next free day; it does not stack. If three
weeks in a row are 0–1 days, start again at the week-1 sets.

## The dose, week by week

Edit **only the sets of the two main lifts** in each workout; the accessories
stay at 3 sets. The reps-in-reserve column is how hard the last set should feel.

| Week | Main lifts | Reps | Reps in reserve | Change in the app |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 3 sets | 8–10 | 3 | nothing, as created |
| 3–5 | 4 sets | 8–12 | 2 | week 3: mains to 4 sets |
| 6–7 | 4 sets | 6–10 | 1–2 | week 6: mains to 6–10 reps |
| 8 | 2 sets | 8–10 | 3 | week 8: mains to 2 sets, lighter. Then re-test (below). |

Weight goes up by the rule the app already has: hit the top of the range on
every set with reps to spare → next step up. Barbell +2.5 kg, cable +5 kg,
dumbbell next pair.

**Re-test in week 8:** back squat, bench, RDL, pulldown, each for one set of
as many good reps as you can at the week-5 weight. History → the exercise's
trend shows its estimated 1-RM, week 1 against week 8. Body weight stays ≤ 82 kg.

## Setting it up in the app

1. Program → **New workout** four times (A, B, C, D) with the exercises above
   in that order; or ask the generator with "lower: jump squat, Pallof, back
   squat, RDL, Bulgarian split squat, leg curl, calves" and fix what it gets
   wrong. Name them A · Lower, B · Pull, C · Push, D · Glutes + arms.
2. In each one, set the sets and reps to the week-1 numbers.
3. Program → **Make a plan**: tap A, B, C, D in that order, pick the days you
   usually train, 8 weeks, start date. Whatever day you train you get the
   next one in order; the days are only a forecast. On a three-day week D
   simply comes round less often.
   E has no place in the order: add it on a Friday by hand when you want it.
4. Keep the old twelve workouts? Move them out of the way: retire the ones you
   will not use so the shelf is short. The history keeps them.
5. Week 3 and week 6: edit the two main lifts in A, B and C as in the table.
